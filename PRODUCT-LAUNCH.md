# Product Launch Plan — Tupua Codex Studio

## Product concept

**Working product:** Tupua Codex Studio + Codex Creator Pack  
**Category:** Digital writing and creator productivity toolkit  
**Audience:** Independent authors, course creators, researchers, coaches and people organizing long-running creative or knowledge projects.

### Positioning

A straightforward workspace to capture ideas, organize drafts, track progress and export a clean manuscript—paired with practical templates that help creators plan, review and prepare their work for sharing.

Do not market it as an AI oracle, a source of supernatural knowledge, a secure vault, legal protection, or a guaranteed path to income.

## What exists today

- A working static browser app in `index.html`.
- The app stores entries only in the visitor's browser profile.
- JSON backup and restore.
- Markdown manuscript export and browser printing.
- Ten creator-planning templates in `CREATOR-PACK.md`.

## Simple first offer

Start with a small digital download rather than a subscription service. The app prototype can be offered as a free companion; the paid item is the organized workbook/templates and a clear getting-started guide.

**Price tests (hypotheses, not market research):**
- Introductory toolkit: US$9–15.
- Standard toolkit after customer feedback: US$19–29.
- Expanded bundle with video walkthroughs and additional examples: US$39–49.

Do not assume these prices will convert. Interview prospective customers, publish a sample page, test one price at a time and track actual sales/refunds before investing in paid ads.

## Draft listing copy

### Title
Tupua Codex Creator Pack — Manuscript Planner, Framework Builder & Research Organizer

### Short description
Bring a big idea into a clear working structure. This creator toolkit includes a lightweight browser-based writing organizer and ten reusable templates for planning manuscripts, documenting frameworks, tracking research, reviewing risks, protecting backups and preparing work for publication.

### What buyers receive
- Access to the browser-based Codex Studio prototype.
- A downloadable set of ten creator templates.
- JSON backup/import workflow and Markdown manuscript export.
- A practical getting-started guide.
- A checklist for preparing a work for publication.

### What it does not include
- Cloud storage, account login or cross-device synchronization.
- Encrypted vault storage or professional records management.
- Personalized legal, financial, medical or publishing advice.
- Guaranteed sales, earnings, protection or publication acceptance.

### Call to action
Start with one idea. Give it a structure. Keep a backup. Build the next version.

## Launch sequence

1. **Protect the source:** keep private manuscripts, unpublished methods, customer details, access tokens and passwords out of this public repository.
2. **Test the app:** use desktop and mobile browsers; create, edit, search, filter, export, import and print sample entries.
3. **Prepare the download:** turn the template pack into clean PDF and editable DOCX/Markdown formats; include a version number and usage guide.
4. **Validate demand:** show a free sample to 10–20 likely users and ask what they would pay for. Do not buy ads before the offer is validated.
5. **Choose a storefront:** compare current fees, payout availability, refund handling, sales-tax/VAT support and account eligibility before opening a seller account.
6. **Publish accurate terms:** state exactly what is included, compatibility, data handling, support response expectations and refund policy.
7. **Track results:** visits, sample downloads, conversion rate, sales, refunds, support requests and customer feedback.

## Sustainable upgrades

Only after users request them:
- Installable offline PWA support.
- Optional encrypted export archive.
- Authenticated cloud sync with clear account deletion and backup controls.
- Collaboration with roles and permissions.
- AI writing assistance only with explicit disclosure, opt-in and careful handling of customer data.

A hosted subscription product needs server-side security, authentication, authorization, encryption, backups, privacy disclosures, support processes and payment-provider integration. Do not collect customer manuscript content until that design is complete.

## Business and legal checks

Before taking money, check product-name availability and trademark risk, business/tax obligations, applicable consumer and privacy rules, accessibility, rights to every included asset, and the storefront's current terms. Use a qualified professional for legal or tax questions. This plan is a practical starting point, not legal, tax or financial advice.

## Financial reality

There is no guarantee this product will earn money. Keep costs near zero, launch a small version, learn from real buyers and reinvest only after sales justify it. Do not borrow money or spend on ads based on projected revenue.
