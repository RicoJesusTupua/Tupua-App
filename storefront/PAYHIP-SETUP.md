# Payhip setup — step by step

This guide prepares the seller. It does not create an account, connect a payout provider, or publish a listing on your behalf.

## 1. Create the store
1. Open https://payhip.com/ and choose the free plan.
2. Create your account and verify the email address.
3. Review account, identity and payout requirements in your own dashboard.
4. Connect a payout provider that is available to you (PayPal and/or Stripe, subject to their eligibility and verification requirements).
5. Do not enter banking details, tax identifiers or passwords into ChatGPT or this repository.

## 2. Add the product
1. In Payhip, open Products and choose Add new product → Digital Product.
2. Upload the customer delivery ZIP, or upload the PDF and DOCX as separate files.
3. Use the title and description from PRODUCT-PAGE-COPY.md.
4. Upload the cover artwork.
5. Set the initial test price to US$9.99 (or choose your local currency after checking payout and tax settings).
6. Save the product as Invisible while reviewing it.

## 3. Review advanced options
- Add a preview file if you want buyers to see a sample before purchase. Use a sample page with no personal or confidential information.
- Do not mark the product tax-exempt unless a qualified tax adviser confirms that is correct.
- Use the ebook tax setting only if the product qualifies under Payhip's guidance and applicable rules.
- Check download limits and PDF stamping if available and appropriate.
- Review the final delivery files from the customer point of view.

## 4. Test before launch
1. Preview the product page on a phone and desktop.
2. Check the title, price, cover, description, file names and spelling.
3. Use Payhip's test purchase method (for example, a 100% off coupon) if you need to test the customer download flow. Check the platform's current instructions first.
4. Confirm the downloaded ZIP opens and contains the PDF, DOCX and read-me.
5. Switch visibility to Unlisted for a soft launch, or Visible when you are ready for public discovery.

## 5. Fees and payouts
Payhip's official pricing page currently lists a Free Forever plan at $0/month plus a 5% transaction fee. PayPal or Stripe processing fees apply separately. Check the live fee schedule and payout provider terms before publishing; fees and eligibility can change.

## 6. Launch carefully
- Start with an organic post to people likely to use the workbook.
- Do not buy ads or borrow money to launch an unvalidated product.
- Track product-page visits, downloads, sales, refunds and customer questions.
- Ask buyers for permission before quoting testimonials or displaying their names.
- Update the product honestly when files change; include a version/date.
