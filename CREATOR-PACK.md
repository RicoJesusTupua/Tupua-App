# Tupua Codex Creator Pack
## A practical workbook for turning ideas into organized, reviewable work

This pack is designed for writers, independent creators, researchers and builders of personal knowledge systems. Use the templates as-is or adapt them. It is an organizational aid, not legal, financial, medical or spiritual authority.

## 6. Ethical and safety review

Before sharing, publishing or deploying a project, ask:

- Could a reader mistake metaphor, belief, simulation or fiction for verified fact?
- Does the work make claims about health, law, money, identity or safety that need qualified review?
- Does it contain personal, confidential, copyrighted or licensed material?
- Have permissions and attribution been checked?
- Could the work encourage surveillance, coercion, discrimination, harassment or unsafe reliance?
- Is there a clear way to correct errors and report harm?
- Can a person opt out, disagree or leave without penalty?

## 7. Publication readiness checklist

- [ ] Title and subtitle are final.
- [ ] Intended audience and scope are clear.
- [ ] Contents and headings are consistent.
- [ ] Claims have sources or are clearly labeled as opinion, belief or creative material.
- [ ] Names, quotations, images and other third-party materials have appropriate permissions.
- [ ] Spelling, grammar and accessibility have been reviewed.
- [ ] A trusted reader has reviewed the work.
- [ ] A clean export and a separate backup exist.
- [ ] Contact, refund and support information is ready for a commercial release.
- [ ] The product description accurately states what buyers receive and what it does not do.

## 9. Backup and access checklist

- [ ] Export a JSON backup after a substantial writing session.
- [ ] Keep at least one backup separate from the device.
- [ ] Use device encryption and a screen lock.
- [ ] Do not use shared or public browser profiles for private work.
- [ ] Never paste passwords, API keys, recovery codes or payment information into a writing workspace.
- [ ] Test that a backup can be imported before relying on it.
- [ ] Keep confidential source material out of public repositories and public demo content.

## Suggested workflow

1. Create a project charter before expanding a large idea.
2. Turn each chapter, feature or principle into its own entry in Codex Studio.
3. Add source notes and mark uncertainty.
4. Review ethical and safety implications before sharing.
5. Export a backup before major edits.
6. Export a Markdown manuscript for editing and publication layout.
7. Keep a version history and a permissions register for commercial work.
