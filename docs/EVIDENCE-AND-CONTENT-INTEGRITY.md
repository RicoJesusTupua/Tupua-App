# Evidence, Biology, and Content Integrity Policy

**Project:** Tupua Codex Studio / Tupua Intelligence System  
**Status:** Development specification  
**Scope:** New and edited content, prompts, generated responses, research notes, and future integrations  
**Effective date:** 2026-11-19

## 1. Evidence-first treatment of extraordinary claims

The system must not present extraterrestrial beings, alien organisms, hidden biological agents, or other extraordinary entities as verified facts without credible, independently checkable evidence. Do not infer the presence of an organism from a name, symbol, feeling, dream, metaphor, coincidence, or unverified report.

When a claim is unsupported, label it as unverified, hypothetical, fictional, or a personal belief as appropriate. Do not convert uncertainty into certainty. Correct errors transparently and preserve an audit trail for material changes.

## 2. Language and content handling

- Do not introduce alien-lifeform or extraterrestrial-lifeform claims into ordinary system narratives, classifications, prompts, or user-facing explanations as factual assertions.
- Remove such references from new or actively maintained content when they are irrelevant or unsupported.
- Preserve legitimate astronomy and astrobiology as evidence-based scientific topics, clearly separated from speculation.
- Preserve historical source records when needed for provenance or auditability; annotate rather than silently rewrite evidence.
- Do not delete user-authored material or historical records without a specific, reviewed scope and backup.

## 3. Substance and contamination safety

The system must never claim that methamphetamine or another substance contains an alien, extraterrestrial, or unusual biological organism without valid laboratory evidence. It must not identify an unknown substance or contamination from appearance, sensation, or narrative context.

If a person is concerned about an unknown substance or possible contamination, advise them not to handle, taste, or consume it, and to seek help from a trusted adult and qualified medical or poison-control professionals. Do not provide instructions for obtaining, preparing, testing, or using illegal drugs.

## 4. Claim provenance and confidence

For factual claims, record where practical:
- the exact claim;
- source and date;
- whether the source is primary or secondary;
- evidence supporting and contradicting it;
- confidence and unresolved uncertainty;
- reviewer and review date.

Distinguish direct observation, independently verified fact, inference, hypothesis, personal belief, metaphor, and fiction. A source citation alone does not guarantee a claim is true.

## 5. Safety, privacy, and governance

- Do not use extraordinary claims to encourage fear, coercion, harassment, discrimination, or unsafe action.
- Do not collect or expose sensitive personal information unnecessarily.
- Require human review for high-impact health, legal, financial, or safety claims.
- Provide a correction path and retain a proportionate change log.
- Keep credentials, private records, and confidential user material out of public repositories.

## 6. Acceptance checks

A change is acceptable only when:
1. New user-facing content does not assert unsupported alien or biological contamination claims as fact.
2. Scientific topics are accurately labeled and separated from speculation.
3. Substance-related content prioritizes safety and qualified help.
4. Historical records are not silently destroyed.
5. Tests or manual review confirm that prompts and templates do not turn unsupported claims into authoritative statements.

## 7. Implementation boundary

This document is a governance specification. It does not establish that every Tupua app, Base44 deployment, prompt, database, export, or external integration has been scanned or updated. Each connected system must be reviewed separately, with changes made on an isolated development branch and verified before any release. No merge or deployment is authorized by this document.
